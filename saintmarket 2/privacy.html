YOUR INFORMATION

Launch-list privacy

A summary of how information submitted through the SaintMarket launch list is used. This covers interest registration, before an app account is created.

Updated 25 October 2026

What you share

The current form asks for your first name, email address, whether you are interested as a resident or business, and your estate or neighbourhood. Businesses also provide a name and may choose service categories. Earlier versions of the form included optional contact and business-research questions; any answers already provided remain part of the launch-list records.

Registration is voluntary. The required fields let us record your interest and send relevant updates. You can browse the website without joining the list. Please do not submit identity documents, payment details or your unit number.

Why we use it

SaintMarket uses these details to record interest, understand which communities and services people want, and communicate relevant launch and access information. Joining the list does not create an account, approve a business or commit you to participation fees.

Who may review it

Authorised SaintMarket organisers manage the list. Where an estate is coordinating participation, its authorised representatives may review relevant registration details. Form responses are not a public business directory.

The form is hosted by Tally, which processes and stores responses on behalf of the form creator. Tally states that form data is stored in Europe, so information submitted from South Africa is processed outside South Africa. Read Tally’s information about form data and its privacy notice for details about its services and providers.

How long we keep it

Our policy is to delete launch-list information 12 months after your latest interaction, unless you join SaintMarket or ask to remain on the list. An interaction means registering, updating your details, replying or expressly renewing your interest. Sending you an update does not restart that period.

If you join the app, the information needed for your account will be covered by the privacy information presented during onboarding. Deleted information may remain in Tally’s backups for up to 90 days under its published deletion process.

Your choices

You may request access to or correction of your information, ask for removal from the launch list, or withdraw your interest in future updates. If you have already been in contact with the SaintMarket team, you can use that existing channel for your request.

A dedicated privacy contact and the formal operator details are still being finalised. This page is a launch-list summary and will be expanded when those details are confirmed. You can also find information about privacy rights and complaints through the Information Regulator of South Africa.
