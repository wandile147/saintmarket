A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you join.

A few details about bringing your business to Amsterdam Estate.

What happens after I register?

You’ll see a confirmation that your business interest is registered. We’ll email you Amsterdam Estate launch updates and explain the business onboarding steps. No further action is needed now.

Who is this registration for?

This launch list is for resident-owned and nearby businesses interested in serving Amsterdam Estate residents. It is a business-interest form; it does not create a resident account or activate a business.

Can a business outside Amsterdam Estate register?

Yes. Nearby businesses that want to serve Amsterdam Estate residents can register alongside resident-owned businesses. Every business needs approval before listing, and continued participation depends on meeting the estate’s standards.

Does registering commit my business to fees?

No. This form records your interest. Applicable fees, payment arrangements and participation terms will be shared for agreement before business activation.

Who is responsible for the services listed?

Each business is responsible for the services it provides. SaintMarket brings discovery, enquiries, bookings and payment details together. Your service descriptions, prices and cancellation terms must be clear to customers.

How are booking or payment issues handled?

Businesses and customers first try to resolve service or appointment issues directly. App problems go to SaintMarket support. Unresolved or serious concerns can be escalated to SaintMarket and Amsterdam Estate’s designated team.

Will Amsterdam Estate facilities be included?

Facility bookings depend on what Amsterdam Estate enables. The estate decides which spaces are available, who may use them and the booking and approval rules. Business registration does not grant access to facilities.
